The evidence ladder

Cannabis gets hyped and it gets feared. Both skip the part that matters: how good is the proof? Every Highbrow video carries one of three tags, right on screen.

STRONG Solid ground

Large randomised controlled trials, or official reviews by bodies like the WHO or a drug regulator. Example: CBD for two rare childhood epilepsies.

MODERATE Promising

Smaller controlled trials or reviews with consistent signals, but not enough to settle it. Example: CBD and anxiety in lab stress tests.

EARLY Too soon to tell

Chart reviews, small or uncontrolled studies, animal work. Interesting, not proof. Example: CBD and sleep.

We also make "what we don't know yet" videos. If the science says "unclear", so do we.
